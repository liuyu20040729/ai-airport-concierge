Terminal Information

Changi Airport has three main terminals (T1, T2, T3), all connected by Skytrain and walkways. Each terminal offers check-in, departure gates, arrival halls, and a wide range of services. Jewel Changi Airport is connected to T1, T2, and T3 via pedestrian bridges.

Check-in

Passengers can check in at airline counters, self-service kiosks, or online. Counters typically open 3 hours before departure and close 45 minutes prior. For Singapore Airlines flights, premium passengers have dedicated check-in areas.

Security & Immigration

Security screening is required for all departing passengers. Automated immigration gates are available for Singapore citizens and registered travellers. Passport control is located after security for international flights.

Passenger Services

Information desks are located in all terminals. Services include: flight enquiries, wayfinding assistance, lost & found, medical aid, and special needs support. Staff are available 24/7.

Jewel Changi Airport

Jewel is a nature-themed entertainment and retail complex connected to T1, T2, and T3. Features the world's tallest indoor waterfall (Rain Vortex), Shiseido Forest Valley, and over 300 retail and dining outlets. Open 24 hours.

Airport Lounges

Multiple lounges available across all terminals: SilverKris Lounge (T3), KrisFlyer Gold Lounge (T2), SATS Premier Lounge (T1), Dnata Lounge (T3), Plaza Premium Lounge (T2), and Marhaba Lounge (T1). Access via business class, frequent flyer status, or pay-per-use.

Taxi

Official taxis available 24/7 at designated stands outside each terminal.

💰 Fare: S$20-35 (approx) to city centre
⏱️ Time: 20-35 minutes
📍 Pickup: Arrival Hall, Level 1 (All Terminals)
💳 Payment: Cash, Credit Card, PayNow

🚇

MRT (Train)

Direct connection to city via East-West Line. Changi Airport MRT station is located in Terminal 3 (Basement 2).

💰 Fare: S$2-3 to city centre
⏱️ Time: ~25 minutes to Tanah Merah / City Hall
🕐 Hours: 05:30 - 23:30 daily
🚉 Station: CG2 Changi Airport (T3)

🚌

Public Bus

Multiple bus routes connect the airport to various parts of Singapore. Bus terminal located at Terminal 2 (Level 1).

💰 Fare: S$2-5 (cash or Ez-Link)
⏱️ Time: 30-60 minutes
🕐 Hours: 06:00 - 23:00
🚏 Routes: 24, 27, 34, 36, 53, 110

🚗

Car Rental

Major car rental companies have counters at the airport. Advance booking recommended.

🚗 Providers: Avis, Hertz, Budget, Sixt, Europcar
📍 Location: Arrival Hall (All Terminals)
🕐 Hours: 06:00 - 00:00
🔑 Requirements: Valid license + Credit Card

🚌 Free Inter-Terminal Shuttle

🕐 Frequency: Every 10 minutes
📍 Pickup: Designated stops at T1, T2, T3
⏱️ Duration: ~5-8 minutes between terminals
🧳 Luggage: Storage space available
♿ Accessibility: Wheelchair-friendly

🚆 Changi Airport Skytrain

🕐 Frequency: Every 2-4 minutes
📍 Location: Airside (after security)
🚉 Stations: T1-T2-T3 (airside)
⏱️ Duration: ~2-3 minutes between terminals
🧳 Luggage: Yes, storage racks available
