AIRPORT GENERAL FAQ

Q: Where can I get airport assistance?
A: Passengers can visit an information desk or speak with airport staff
for assistance.

Q: Where can I find transportation information?
A: Transportation information is available at airport information
desks and designated transport areas.

Q: Is Wi-Fi available?
A: Free Wi-Fi is available throughout the passenger terminal.

Q: Where can I find a taxi?
A: Official taxis are available at the designated taxi pickup area.
Passengers should follow airport signs.

Q: Where can I get help with lost property?
A: Passengers should contact the airport's Lost and Found service or
an information desk.

Q: How can passengers get accessibility assistance?
A: Passengers requiring accessibility assistance should contact airport
staff or an information desk.
