AIRPORT WAYFINDING INFORMATION

General Wayfinding

Passengers should follow the airport's official directional signs throughout the terminal.

Common signs include:
- Check-in
- Security
- Departure Gates
- Arrivals
- Baggage Claim
- Ground Transportation
- Taxi
- Information Desk
- Restrooms
- Immigration
- Customs

Baggage Claim

After arriving from the aircraft, passengers should follow signs marked "Baggage Claim" or "Arrivals".

The baggage claim area is located in the arrivals section of the terminal.

Passengers can also ask airport information staff for assistance.

Taxi Area

Passengers looking for official taxis should follow signs marked "Taxi" or "Ground Transportation".

The taxi pickup area is located outside the terminal in the designated ground transportation area.

Passengers should use the official designated taxi pickup area.

Information Desk

Passengers who need general assistance should follow signs marked "Information" or "Information Desk".

Airport information staff can help passengers with general airport enquiries and directions.

Restrooms

Passengers should follow signs marked "Restrooms", "Toilets", or the appropriate accessibility symbol.

Restrooms are generally located throughout passenger terminal areas.

Accessibility

Passengers requiring wheelchair assistance or other accessibility support should contact airport information staff or the appropriate airport assistance service.

General Navigation Guidance

When providing directions, give simple step-by-step instructions.

If the exact location cannot be confirmed from the available airport information, do not invent a terminal number, gate number, floor number, or distance.

Instead, advise the passenger to follow official airport signs or contact airport information staff.
