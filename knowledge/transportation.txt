AIRPORT TRANSPORTATION

Taxi
Official taxi services are available at the designated taxi pickup
area outside the terminal.

Ride-hailing
Ride-hailing services may be available at designated pickup zones.
Passengers should follow airport signs for the approved pickup area.

Public Transport
Passengers may use public buses, trains, or metro services depending
on the airport's transport connections.

Airport Shuttle
Airport shuttle buses may connect the terminal with nearby transport
hubs, hotels, parking areas, or other airport facilities.

Car Rental
Car rental services may be available through rental counters or
designated pickup locations.

Parking
The airport provides short-term and long-term parking areas.
Passengers should follow airport signs to the appropriate parking
facility.

Passengers should check the airport's official transportation
information or contact airport staff for current schedules,
availability, and pickup locations.
