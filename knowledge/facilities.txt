AIRPORT FACILITIES

Information Desk
The airport has information desks located in the main terminal.
Passengers can ask airport staff for assistance with directions,
airport services, facilities, and general enquiries.

Restrooms
Public restrooms are available throughout the terminal.
Accessible restrooms are available for passengers with reduced mobility.

Wi-Fi
Free Wi-Fi is available throughout the passenger terminal.
Passengers can connect to the airport's public Wi-Fi network.

Charging Stations
Charging points are available in selected seating areas near the
departure gates and passenger waiting areas.

ATMs
ATMs are available in the main terminal near the departure and
arrival areas.

Food and Beverage
Passengers can find restaurants, cafes, and convenience stores
within the terminal. Opening hours may vary.

Shopping
Retail shops and duty-free stores are available in selected
departure areas.

Lost and Found
Passengers who lose personal belongings should contact the airport's
Lost and Found service or the information desk.

First Aid
First-aid assistance is available at the airport. Passengers who
require urgent medical assistance should contact airport staff
immediately.

Accessibility
Wheelchair assistance and accessible facilities are available for
passengers with reduced mobility. Passengers requiring assistance
should contact airport staff or the information desk.
